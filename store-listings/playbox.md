## Full description (4000 max)

```
Five games that have nothing in common except that they are all yours after
one download, and all of them work with the network off.

ECHO — See with sound
A maze you cannot see. Tap and a ring of sound spreads out, lighting up every
wall it touches for about three seconds. Then the dark takes it back. Your
pings are limited, some hazards make no light at all, and you find those by
sound alone. Sixty levels. Headphones make this one.

STARFALL — Slingshot the galaxy
You never steer. You drag back, release, and real gravity from every planet
on screen bends the flight. Aim past a planet instead of at your target and
let the planet do the turning. Chain gravity assists to climb further. Endless.

PRISM — Split the light
White light goes in; every crystal wants a different colour. Prisms split
white into red, green and blue. Filters subtract. Combiners put colour back
together. Forty-five hand-built levels, and the par printed on each one is
the proven minimum, so three stars is a real claim.

VORTEX — Don't touch the walls
You orbit a burning core while rings of debris collapse inward, each with a
gap somewhere in it. Be in the gap. Eight escalating zones: rotating rings,
shutters that close as they fall, phantom rings you hear before you see.
Every ring is checked as physically reachable before it spawns.

DAILY LOCK — One lock a day
Five dials, seven glyphs, six tries, and everyone on earth gets the same lock
each day. There are no words in it at all, so it plays identically in every
language, and the marks are shapes rather than colours, so it works with any
form of colour blindness.

ONE CHALLENGE A DAY, ACROSS ALL FIVE
Every morning Playbox picks three goals from three different games — clear
three Echo levels, reach 400 light years in Starfall, three-star a Prism
puzzle. Finish all three and your streak grows. The challenge is the same for
everyone, generated from the date.

Goals pay tokens, and tokens spend anywhere: extra pings in Echo, hints in
Prism, a revive in Vortex, a dial reveal in Daily Lock. Nothing is locked
behind them and nothing expires.

BUILT SMALL AND QUIET
• Plays fully offline. No account, no sign-up, nothing to log into.
• A few hundred kilobytes of game. No downloads after install.
• One thumb, portrait, any length of session.
• Free, funded by ads — but no ad ever appears during play, none in the first
  45 seconds, and every game is completable without watching one.
• Reduce-motion setting for Vortex.

MORE GAMES ARE COMING
Playbox is built to grow. New games arrive as app updates and appear on the
shelf with everything else — no separate download, and your tokens and streak
carry straight over.
```

## Data safety — the declaration that matches what the app actually does
- Collects or shares required user data? **Yes**
- Data type: **Device or other IDs** — collected **and** shared
- Purpose: **Advertising or marketing**
- Processed ephemerally? **No** · Required or optional? **Required**
- Encrypted in transit? **Yes**
- Deletion: point at the advertising-ID reset in Android settings, which is
  what the privacy policy describes
- App activity, personal info, location, photos, files, contacts, messages:
  **No.** Progress, tokens and streaks never leave the device.
