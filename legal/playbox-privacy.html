Privacy Policy

Playbox: Mind & Reflex Games  ·  package com.yourstudio.playbox
Last updated: 2026-08-31

Playbox: Mind & Reflex Games (“the app”) is published by Your Studio (“we”, “us”). This page explains what the app does and does not do with information about you.

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device.
  • The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google collects some information in order to serve those ads.
  • We ourselves never see or receive any personal data about you.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress — levels completed, best scores, daily-challenge goals, streaks, tokens and your sound and motion settings — in local storage inside the app on your device. This never leaves your device, is never sent to us, is not backed up to any server, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The daily challenge is generated from the calendar date on your device, not from a server, and no request is made to obtain it. That is why everyone gets the same challenge without the app needing to know who you are.

Advertising

The app is free and is funded by advertising served through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and approximate region;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived;
  • information about ads shown to you, such as whether an ad was viewed or clicked.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. Google's description of how it uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about advertising

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads. You can reset the identifier or delete it entirely, which stops it being used to personalise ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a Google-certified consent message the first time you open it, and your choice is respected. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the app's settings.
  • Non-personalised ads. If you decline consent, ads are served without personalisation.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET — required to fetch advertisements. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • AD_ID — access to the advertising identifier, used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app requests no other permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, files or call logs.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone. The only third party involved is Google, in its role as our advertising provider, as described above.

Data retention

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's own retention policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it. As we hold no personal data ourselves, such requests should be directed to Google. You can still contact us and we will help you find the right route.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, note it in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about the app: you@example.com
