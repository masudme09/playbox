How to play

      
Five dials, seven glyphs, six tries. Everyone on earth gets the same lock each day.

      

      
Tumblers. Every lock also states one structural rule up front — mirrored, ascending, all different and so on. The secret always obeys it, and so must your guesses. That is what makes six tries enough.

      
Accessibility. Marks are shapes, not colours: a solid ring, a hollow ring, or a dim glyph. The seven glyphs differ in outline as well as hue, so the game is fully playable with any form of colour blindness.
